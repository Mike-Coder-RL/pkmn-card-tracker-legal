Privacy Policy

Pkmn Card Tracker · Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy explains what the Pkmn Card Tracker app ("the app") does with information. In short: the app has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking, and your collection stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

The cards you mark as owned (including normal and reverse holo checkmarks) are saved only on your device. We do not receive or store your collection on any server. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or advertising identifier. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

Card data from third parties

To show sets and card images, the app requests public card data from the Pokémon TCG API (pokemontcg.io). Your device's IP address is visible to that service as with any internet request. We do not send it any information about you or your collection. Please see their website for their own practices.

Subscriptions and purchases

Access to the app is provided through an auto-renewable subscription. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. To confirm whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier and purchase and subscription status information, and may receive device and app version details needed to operate the service. This is used only to unlock the features you paid for and to prevent fraud. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Sharing and exporting

If you use the app's export feature, the app opens your device's share sheet so you can send your list wherever you choose. We do not receive that data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your choices

You can delete your data at any time by deleting the app. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID settings (Settings > your name > Subscriptions).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
